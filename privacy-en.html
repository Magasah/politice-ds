About this policy

This policy describes data handling in Dushanbe Drift version 1.0.0. The developer name provided for the game is MCode Academy. Privacy contact: muhammadrlx777@gmail.com.

This description is based on a review of the current game. If its features or data practices change, this policy must be updated alongside the app.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a local profile: your chosen nickname, selected and owned vehicles, earned game coins, scores, distance records, reward history and gameplay preferences. A nickname may contain personal information; avoid entering sensitive information.

Tuning, button layout, controls, language, audio, graphics and game-camera preferences are also stored locally. Backup and recovery files may retain earlier values.

When you use photo mode, the game saves images of the game world in its app folder. It does not use your device’s physical camera.

How data are used

Local saves let you continue playing, customize vehicles and track progress and rewards. The game reads RAM capacity, graphics-memory capacity and processor count to select graphics quality. Screen and safe-area information and touch input support the interface and controls; local date and time support daily rewards and game-photo filenames.

No access to advertising or persistent device identifiers, contacts, location, microphone input or physical camera images was found in the reviewed game code.

Transmission and third-party components

No functionality uploading local profiles, saves or game photos to a developer server or sharing these data with other organizations was found in the reviewed version.

Unity Analytics and cloud crash reporting are disabled. The Unity engine and Android may produce local diagnostic logs; no game-implemented upload of those logs was found.

Operating-system and app-store services operate independently and may have their own data practices. The absence of network permissions in the game does not by itself establish the absence of platform processing.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The reviewed version has no advertising integration, online accounts, online gameplay service or real-money payments. Its player profile is local. Buying vehicles means spending earned in-game DC coins.

Retention and deletion

Local data remain until overwritten or removed. No automatic deletion period is configured. Saves are not encrypted by the game; access protection depends on your device and operating system.

Delete data on Android
Settings → Apps → Dushanbe Drift → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage.

This permanently removes progress and preferences and is intended to clear app-scoped files, including game photos. Labels and behaviour may vary by device; check the result. Uninstalling normally also removes app-scoped data.

Independently copied photos and external backups must be removed separately. The game has no complete in-game profile erasure button. There is no online account or server profile to delete.

Children and families

The developer states that the game is intended for a broad audience, including children under 13. The local features described here also apply to children. The reviewed version has no ads, online accounts or built-in real-money payments.

Parents and guardians can manage local data through device settings and use the contact below. This audience description is not a Google Play content rating or a certification of Families compliance.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its code contains no added analytics, advertising trackers, data collection forms or cookies. No login is needed to read this policy.

When you visit, GitHub processes web requests as the hosting provider and may record your IP address and technical visit information. This is separate from data stored inside the game. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy and data-deletion questions, contact muhammadrlx777@gmail.com — MCode Academy.

Sending an email is a separate action you choose: email services transmit your sender address and message content to the recipient. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. The game does not send such emails automatically.
